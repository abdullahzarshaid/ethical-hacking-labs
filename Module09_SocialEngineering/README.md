# Module 09 - Social Engineering

**In one line.** Attack the human layer - and learn to defend it.

**Why it matters.** Most breaches start with a person, not an exploit. Phishing is the number-one initial-access vector.

**Key concepts.** Phishing, spear phishing, pretexting, baiting, vishing, tailgating, and the psychology of influence.

**Core tools.** Gophish and the Social-Engineer Toolkit (SET) - in your lab only, against your own test accounts.

**Practise in your lab.** Build a phishing page in an isolated lab against a test account, and study the tells that give it away.

**Defender's view.** Awareness training, MFA, email filtering, and a verification culture.

> New here? Start with the [lab setup guide](../LAB-SETUP.md) and work the modules in order.
