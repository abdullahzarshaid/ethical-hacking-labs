# Module 10 - Denial of Service

**In one line.** Understand how availability is attacked at the network and application layer.

**Why it matters.** Availability is one third of the CIA triad; knowing DoS mechanics is key to detection and mitigation.

**Key concepts.** Volumetric vs protocol vs application-layer DoS, DDoS, amplification, SYN floods, slowloris.

**Core tools.** hping3, slowhttptest, and the concepts behind LOIC/HOIC - in an isolated lab only.

**Practise in your lab.** Flood a lab target and watch resource exhaustion. Never test bandwidth attacks on shared or real networks.

**Defender's view.** Rate limiting, a WAF, upstream DDoS protection, and capacity planning.
